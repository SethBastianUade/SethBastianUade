## Conventions

Each notebook follows the same structure:

- **Summary** — what the topic is, in one paragraph.
- **Current understanding** — where my mental model is today.
- **Resources** — reading queue, checked off when read.
- **Notes** — dated entries, appended over time.
- **TODO** — concrete next steps.
- **Open questions** — what I cannot yet answer.

## Status legend

- `Reading` — actively studying; notes are preliminary.
- `Drafting` — summary is stable, notes are organised.
- `Working` — running experiments or implementations.
- `Revisited` — revisited after time away, updated.
